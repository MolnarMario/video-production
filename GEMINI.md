# Autonomous Execution ("YOLO Mode")

## Operating Guidelines
- **Autonomous Action**: Execute tasks directly and proactively. Do not pause to ask for permission or confirmation before creating files, editing code, running terminal commands, or executing tests.
- **Decision Making**: Make reasonable engineering and architectural decisions automatically. Favor progress and rapid iteration.
- **Self-Healing & Iteration**: When errors, lint issues, or failing tests arise, diagnose and fix them immediately without waiting for user intervention.
- **Direct Output**: Keep responses concise and focused on what was done and what the current status is.
